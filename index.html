A physical bridge for the wallet era.

UDIR is being designed for OEMs, system integrators and professional teams that need to bring supported digital credentials into real-world workflows.

It establishes a secure session with a user’s smartphone, receives authorised credential data and passes a verification result to the connected system.

Design principles

Less data.
More control.

01

Local-first

Designed to verify supported credentials directly on the device, without a mandatory external verification server.

02

Privacy by design

Request only the attributes a workflow needs, including age-threshold outcomes where the credential supports them.

03

Offline capable

Continue selected workflows without continuous connectivity, subject to trust-data freshness and credential status.

04

OEM ready

Compact construction, standard interfaces and configurable software for new and existing equipment.

FAQ

Before the
first pilot.

Clear answers about what UDIR is — and what it is not yet.

Is UDIR available to purchase?+

Not yet. UDIR is currently a product concept in development and market validation.

Does it require internet or a cloud service?+

UDIR is designed for offline verification in compatible scenarios and without a mandatory cloud service. Periodic connectivity may still be needed for trust updates or credential-status checks.

Are the listed standards already certified?+

No certification is claimed. Support and conformance will be confirmed through development, interoperability testing and regulatory assessment.

Can it integrate with existing equipment?+

That is a primary design objective. The concept uses USB-C, a documented device protocol and an optional SDK to fit OEM workflows.

Can it perform age verification?+

Privacy-preserving age-threshold verification is planned where supported by the credential. Deployment remains subject to applicable national regulation.
